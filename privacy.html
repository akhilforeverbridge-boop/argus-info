ARGUS Privacy Policy

ARGUS is a private email-processing automation operated by the application owner.

Information accessed

ARGUS accesses selected Gmail messages and Gmail labels after receiving authorization from the mailbox owner.

How information is used

Gmail information is used only to identify and process relevant messages, update authorized workflow records, and apply labels that indicate message-processing status.

Data sharing

ARGUS does not sell Gmail information or use Gmail information for advertising. Information is processed only as required to operate the authorized automation.

Data retention and security

ARGUS retains only information required for the associated workflow. Access credentials are stored as protected repository secrets and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Revoking access

The mailbox owner may revoke ARGUS access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the support email displayed on the Google authorization screen.
